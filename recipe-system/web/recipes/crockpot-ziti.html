Crockpot Baked Ziti

Layered crockpot baked ziti where the penne goes in uncooked and cooks right in the sauce. Layers of marinara, a basil-ricotta filling, and parmesan, finished with melted mozzarella. Feeds a crowd (needs a 6-quart or larger slow cooker) and works well for a late Thursday dinner, meal prep, and weekend batch cooking.

Preparation Steps

1
Stir the ricotta, eggs, and chopped basil together in a bowl until evenly combined.
2
In a large bowl, mix both jars of pasta sauce with the tomato sauce. You will use this in three roughly equal portions (about 2 1/3 cups each).
3
Grate the parmesan, and split the uncooked penne into three equal piles so the layers cook evenly.
4
Grease or spray the inside of a 6-quart (or larger) slow cooker.
5
First layer: spread about 2 1/3 cups of sauce over the bottom, scatter one pile of penne over it, spoon on half of the ricotta mixture and spread it out, then sprinkle with 1 cup of parmesan.
6
Second layer: repeat with another 2 1/3 cups of sauce, the second pile of penne, the rest of the ricotta mixture, and the remaining 1 cup of parmesan.
7
Top with the last pile of penne and cover it completely with the remaining sauce, spreading it so no noodles are left dry.
8
Cover and cook on HIGH for 2-3 hours (about 2 1/2 hours is usually right) until the pasta is al dente. On LOW, plan on roughly 3-4 hours and check so the noodles don't turn mushy.
9
For the final 15-30 minutes, sprinkle the mozzarella over the top, cover, and let it melt.
10
Serve as is or with red pepper flakes, garlic bread, roasted vegetables, or a side salad.
11
Notes: A full batch will not fit in a 4-quart slow cooker; halve the recipe for that size. Cottage cheese or cream cheese can stand in for the ricotta, and asiago can replace the mozzarella. Browned Italian sausage can be added for a meatier version. Leftovers keep 4-5 days in an airtight container in the fridge.
