Crockpot Mexican Quinoa Tacos

Easy meatless (or vegan) crockpot quinoa tacos — dump everything in the slow cooker, no chopping or sautéing required. Flavor-packed with black beans, fire-roasted tomatoes, enchilada sauce, corn, and taco seasoning. Nutritious plant-based protein perfect for taco night or meal prep. Great vegetarian option alongside your chicken recipes.

Preparation Steps

1
Rinse quinoa if needed. Generously spray a 6-quart slow cooker with cooking spray (or use a liner).
2
Add quinoa, stock, black beans, tomatoes (undrained), enchilada sauce, corn, and taco seasoning to the crockpot. Stir well to combine.
3
Cover and cook on HIGH for 2.5–4 hours until quinoa has popped and absorbed most liquid (do not use low setting).
4
Stir gently, taste, and adjust seasoning with salt/pepper if needed.
5
Warm tortillas. Fill with quinoa mixture and load up with favorite toppings (lime, avocado, cilantro, cheese, etc.).
6
For meal prep: Store filling separately (refrigerate up to 4 days; reheat with splash of water). Assemble tacos fresh. Excellent meatless option for variety in weekly plans.
