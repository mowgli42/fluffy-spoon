Filipino Chicken Adobo (Crockpot Version)

Authentic Filipino Chicken Adobo made easy in the slow cooker. Tangy, savory, garlicky perfection with vinegar, soy sauce, and bay leaves. Perfect for meal prep, weekend batch cooking, or as a base for lunches. Stovetop option also available but crockpot is set-it-and-forget-it.

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

1
Optional but recommended: Combine chicken, vinegar, water, soy sauce, garlic, bay leaves, and pepper in a bowl. Marinate in fridge overnight or at least 20-30 min.
2
Place chicken and all marinade ingredients into the slow cooker.
3
Cover and cook on LOW for 6 hours or HIGH for 3 hours until chicken is very tender.
4
Remove chicken to a plate. Pour sauce into a skillet or saucepan, bring to a boil, and reduce by about half (5-10 min) to thicken and concentrate flavors. (Optional but recommended for best taste).
5
Shred or chop chicken and return to reduced sauce in crockpot or bowl. Stir to coat well.
6
Serve over rice or quinoa. For meal prep: Divide into containers with rice and veggies for grab-and-go lunches. Excellent flavor that improves next day.
