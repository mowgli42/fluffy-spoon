Crockpot Creamy Chicken + Rice

Nutritious crockpot comfort food — seared chicken with brown rice, veggies, and a creamy Greek yogurt–Dijon finish. Convenient clean-eating dinner with leftovers for meal prep.

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

1
Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2
Add garlic and cook 1 minute until fragrant.
3
Add chicken and sear 2-3 minutes until golden but not fully cooked.
4
Season with salt and pepper; add garlic and chicken to the slow cooker.
5
Stir in rice, carrots, onions, corn, and peas.
6
Add broth and stir. Cook on HIGH 3-4 hours or LOW 6-8 hours.
7
Whisk Greek yogurt and Dijon together.
8
When cooking is done, stir in yogurt-mustard mixture; season to taste.
9
Serve warm with sliced green onions.
