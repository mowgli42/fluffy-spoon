Easy Rosemary Garlic White Bean Soup

Eight-ingredient rosemary garlic white bean soup with rich, bold flavor — quick stovetop weeknight soup (vegan-friendly with veg broth).

Ingredients

Preparation Steps

1
Purée one can of cannellini beans with its liquid until smooth; drain the other two cans.
2
Mince garlic; sauté in olive oil over medium ~1 minute until fragrant.
3
Add puréed beans, drained beans, broth, rosemary, thyme, crushed red pepper, and black pepper; stir.
4
Cover, bring to a boil over medium-high; then medium-low, uncover, simmer 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
5
Smash beans slightly to thicken; salt to taste if needed. Serve hot with crusty bread.
