Baked Chicken Breasts

Tender, juicy baked chicken breasts seasoned with a simple five-ingredient pantry rub and roasted at high heat. Five minutes of prep and under 20 minutes in the oven for an easy weeknight dinner.

Preparation Steps

1
Preheat oven to 450°F. Pat the chicken dry with paper towels, then pound the breasts to an even thickness of about 1 inch.
2
Pour the olive oil into a 13x9-inch baking dish. Coat the chicken lightly in the oil, then arrange the breasts side by side in the dish, alternating direction so they fit without overlapping. Use a dish rated for high heat.
3
In a small bowl, whisk together the salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and smoked paprika. Sprinkle over both sides of the chicken and rub it in with your hands.
4
Bake 15 to 20 minutes, until an instant-read thermometer inserted into the thickest part registers 160°F. Larger or thicker breasts will need longer.
5
Tent with foil and rest 5 to 10 minutes before slicing. The temperature will rise to 165°F as it sits. Slice against the grain and spoon the pan juices over the top.
