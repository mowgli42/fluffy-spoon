Instant Pot Cheesy Cauliflower Potato Soup

Easy gluten-free one-pot Instant Pot dinner — creamy potato, cheese, and bacon with cauliflower for fiber and a lighter bowl.

Preparation Steps

1
Set Instant Pot to SAUTE (use + adjust as needed).
2
Add bacon and garlic; cook ~4 minutes until bacon lightly browned. Cook enough bacon to reserve some for garnish if desired.
3
Drain grease; set aside garnish bacon.
4
Add potatoes, cauliflower, and chicken stock. Seal lid/vent.
5
MANUAL high pressure 10 minutes. Turn pot off.
6
Natural release 10 minutes, then quick release until fully depressurized.
7
Add milk and 1 1/4 cup cheese. Mash with potato masher or immersion blender to desired consistency; add more broth if needed.
8
Serve topped with remaining cheese and bacon crumbles.
