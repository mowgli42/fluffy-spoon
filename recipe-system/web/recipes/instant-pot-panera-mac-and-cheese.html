Instant Pot Panera Mac and Cheese

Easy Instant Pot copycat of Panera's mac and cheese — creamy white cheddar and American cheese with a touch of Dijon, ready in under 20 minutes.

Preparation Steps

1
Place pasta, water, salt, and butter in Instant Pot; stir. Seal. Pressure/manual 4 minutes (~10 min to pressurize).
2
Quick-release carefully. If water spurts, close valve briefly, wait ~15s, release in short intervals until float drops.
3
If significant water remains, gently pour most off (keep a colander handy).
4
Stir in milk, white cheddar, American cheese; if drained, add butter/salt to taste. Stir in Dijon; optional pepper and hot sauce.
5
Use Warm to melt cheese if needed. For less creamy, start with 1/2 cup milk. Serve.
